## Risk Items Requiring Human Review

1. **MIL-STD-810 Compliance**: Only the LNA (NC1020-1212) and limiter (LMLPF-BV-0+) are specified with military screening. For full compliance with MIL-STD-810 operating temperature range (-55°C to +125°C), all components should be verified for operation at these extremes, or military-grade alternatives should be selected.

2. **FCC Part 15 Certification**: While individual components meet EMC requirements, the complete system must be tested and certified per FCC Part 15 rules. The high-speed ADC clock and wideband RF front-end may require additional filtering and shielding.

3. **Power Supply Sequencing**: The design requires proper power sequencing for RF components and ADC, which is not specified in the component selection. This should be reviewed to prevent latch-up or damage during power-up.

4. **REACH Compliance**: While no hazardous substances are identified in the datasheets, a full substance declaration should be obtained from all suppliers for REACH compliance.

## Recommendations

1. For MIL-STD-810 temperature range compliance:
   - Replace commercial ADC with military-grade variant (ATR1222)
   - Verify all components specified for operation at -55°C to +125°C
   - Consider military-grade versions of power supplies

2. For complete system compliance:
   - Implement proper RF shielding for the wideband design
   - Add EMI filtering on all power inputs
   - Implement careful power sequencing circuitry

3. For documentation:
   - Obtain RoHS and REACH declarations from all component suppliers
   - Prepare for FCC Part 15 testing of the complete system
   - Document component traceability for MIL-STD requirements if applicable

The design shows good compliance selection for most requirements, with particular attention to RF performance and low-noise characteristics. However, additional attention should be given to military screening and system-level EMC considerations for full compliance with the stated requirements.
