## Risk Items Requiring Human Review

1. **High-Frequency RF Layout**: The 5-18 GHz frequency range requires extremely precise PCB layout with controlled impedance traces and proper grounding. This may require expert review to ensure FCC Part 15 compliance.

2. **Power Supply Decoupling**: Multiple power supplies with different requirements need careful decoupling strategy to prevent noise coupling between analog and digital sections.

3. **EMI Shielding**: The industrial temperature range and wide RF bandwidth require proper shielding and EMI mitigation measures to meet CE marking requirements.

4. **Thermal Management**: With 20W total power dissipation in a compact volume (100-200 cm³), thermal design must be verified to ensure reliable operation across the temperature range.

## Recommendations

1. **All components appear compliant** with the specified standards (RoHS, REACH, FCC Part 15, CE Marking). No alternatives are necessary for compliance reasons.

2. **FCC/CE Testing**: Prioritize FCC Part 15 and CE marking testing during prototyping, especially focusing on the RF front-end and high-speed digital sections.

3. **Environmental Considerations**: While RoHS compliant, some components (GaAs-based RF components) require special handling during disposal. Document proper disposal procedures.

4. **Documentation**: Obtain and maintain compliance documentation from all component manufacturers, including RoHS declarations and REACH SVHC statements.

5. **Production Testing**: Implement compliance testing during production to ensure continued compliance with all applicable standards.
