## Risk Items Requiring Human Review

1. **High-Frequency Performance**: The 5-18 GHz operating frequency range may pose challenges for EMC compliance. Additional testing may be required to verify radiated emissions.

2. **Power Supply Noise**: The wideband RF components are sensitive to power supply noise. The LT8645S buck converter's switching frequency may require careful filtering to prevent interference with sensitive analog circuits.

3. **Interconnect Compliance**: The SMA/2.4mm connectors must be properly installed to maintain impedance matching across the frequency range to avoid signal degradation.

4. **Thermal Management**: The TGA4943-SL LNA has a high P1dB rating of 33 dBm, which may require thermal considerations at high power levels.

## Recommendations

1. **EMC Testing**: Plan for pre-compliance EMC testing to address potential issues with radiated emissions, especially at the upper end of the frequency range.

2. **Power Supply Design**: Implement low-noise power supply filtering for the RF components to prevent power supply noise from degrading system performance.

3. **PCB Layout**: Use best practices for RF PCB layout, including controlled impedance traces, proper grounding, and shielding where necessary.

4. **Connector Installation**: Ensure proper torque and soldering procedures for the SMA connectors to maintain RF performance.

5. **Environmental Testing**: Consider environmental testing (temperature, humidity) to validate performance across the specified operating range.

6. **Documentation**: Maintain documentation for all components, including RoHS and REACH compliance certificates, to support CE marking requirements.

7. **Functional Safety**: For any potential safety-critical applications, consider implementing functional safety measures and relevant standards.
