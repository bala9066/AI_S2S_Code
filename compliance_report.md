## Risk Items Requiring Human Review

1. **Radar Frequency Range (300-1000 MHz)**: While components are individually compliant, the entire system operates in the UHF radar band which may require special regulatory considerations in certain regions. Validate local regulations for radar operation in this frequency range.

2. **High Transmit Power (+20 dBm max input)**: Although the components are rated for this power level, ensure proper RF shielding and ESD protection measures are implemented to prevent interference with other electronic systems.

3. **Military Standards**: The design indicates compliance with MIL-STD-461 and MIL-STD-810, but formal qualification testing would be required for actual military certification.

4. **Thermal Management**: With power consumption up to 15W in a compact module, verify adequate thermal design to prevent component overheating during extended operation at maximum power.

## Recommendations

1. **No non-compliant components identified**: All selected components meet the required standards for the intended application.

2. **For production**, ensure:
   - All components are procured through authorized distributors with RoHS/REACH documentation
   - Final assembly processes comply with RoHS requirements (lead-free soldering)
   - Full system testing includes radiated emissions compliance verification
   - Military compliance documentation is maintained for all components

3. **Design Enhancements**:
   - Add LC filters on DC-DC converter input/output to meet FCC Part 15 requirements
   - Implement proper RF shielding for the high-gain LNA stage
   - Include temperature monitoring circuitry for military operation

4. **Documentation**:
   - Maintain a bill of materials with compliance documentation for all components
   - Prepare technical file for CE marking declaration
   - Document any variances from standard RoHS exemptions (for military applications)
