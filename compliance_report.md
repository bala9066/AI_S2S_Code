## Risk Items Requiring Human Review

1. **Military Compliance Documentation**
   - While components are rated for military temperatures, full MIL-STD-883 qualification documentation is not available
   - Recommendation: Request manufacturer's military datasheets and screening certificates

2. **EMC Testing Integration**
   - No conducted or radiated emission testing has been performed
   - Recommendation: Perform full system-level EMC testing including:
     - EN 55032 for conducted and radiated emissions
     - EN 55035 for immunity testing
     - IEC 61000-4-x series for ESD, EFT, and surge immunity

3. **Defense Supply Chain Security**
   - For defense applications, verify components are sourced from authorized distributors
   - Recommendation: Implement AS6081 compliant supplier management program

## Recommendations for Non-Compliant Components

No components are outright non-compliant, but the following recommendations are provided to ensure full compliance:

1. **For RoHS-exempt components with lead terminations:**
   - Maintain proper documentation of RoHS exemptions per 2011/65/EU Annex III, 7a
   - Consider alternative lead-free processes where possible

2. **For Military applications:**
   - Verify lot traceability and screening certificates for each component
   - Implement additional testing per MIL-STD-883 Method 5004/5005/5007

3. **For CE Marking:**
   - Ensure all necessary technical documentation is prepared
   - Conduct EMC testing as part of a Notified Body assessment if required

4. **For FCC Part 15:**
   - Perform system-level testing to ensure compliance
   - Implement appropriate shielding and filtering for clock distribution and LVDS outputs

This compliance report assumes the system will be classified as electronic measurement equipment (Category A) for CE marking purposes, which would require compliance with EN 61326-1. Different classifications may apply based on final system configuration and intended use.
