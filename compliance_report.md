# Compliance Report: Project rf78 (2.4 GHz Bluetooth PA)

**Project:** rf78
**Description:** 2.4 GHz Bluetooth RF power amplifier module (10 W output, 28 V supply)
**Date:** October 26, 2023

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Compliance Matrix

The **rf78** hardware design is primarily targeted at Industrial/BLE applications.
*   **RoHS / REACH:** The design utilizes standard electronic components (Silicon/GaAs). Compliance is anticipated based on industry-standard BOMs, though vendor confirmation is required for the discrete FET and Passive components.
*   **EMC (FCC/CE):** The design involves a high-power (+40 dBm) switching amplifier. While the components themselves are passive or standard ICs, the **system-level integration** requires significant review to prevent radiated spurious emissions, especially given the harmonics generated by a non-linear PA. The use of a 555 timer for biasing introduces a low-frequency clock source that must be shielded.
*   **Safety / Environmental:** The design operates at 28 V and dissipates approximately 20–25 W of heat. Compliance relies heavily on the thermal design (Heatsink) and clearance/creepage for the 28 V rail.

## 3. Detailed Compliance & Risk Assessment

### Functional Safety Risks
1.  **VSWR Mismatch Failure:** The custom LPF and Output connector are critical failure points under VSWR 3:1.
    *   *Mitigation:* Verify voltage ratings of all capacitors immediately after the PA output. Use 500V rated C0G/NP0 ceramics.
2.  **Thermal Runaway:** The requirement is -40 to +85°C ambient.
    *   *Calculation:* 20W Dissipation + 1.5°C/W Heatsink = 30°C Rise. Case Temp ≈ 115°C (at 85°C Ambient).
    *   *Result:* **FAIL**. The component will exceed the +105°C PCB shutdown threshold at maximum ambient temperature before the protection triggers (or will trigger erroneously).
    *   *Fix:* Heatsink needs to be significantly larger (e.g., <1.0°C/W) or forced air (fan) is required.

### Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) Risks
1.  **Harmonics:** A 10W Non-linear PA generates strong 2nd (4.8 GHz) and 3rd (7.2 GHz) harmonics.
    *   *Requirement:* -30 dBc.
    *   *Risk:* The 5th-order filter may be insufficient if the filter components saturate or have poor Q. The PCB layout filter section must be shielded.
2.  **Spurious Emissions (555 Timer):** The use of an LM555 for bias sequencing is an EMC violation risk for a sensitive receiver (Bluetooth).
    *   *Fix:* Replace with RC-based discrete MOSFET logic or a dedicated low-noise sequencer IC.

---

## 4. Final Recommendations

To achieve a **PASS** status for Industrial/RoHS/EMC standards, the following changes are mandatory:

1.  **Replace PA (QPF4528):** Use the **CGRM2812** or **TGF2995**. The selected part is fundamentally under-powered.
2.  **Replace Sequencer (LM555):** Remove the oscillator-based timer. Use a delay circuit based on RC charging of MOSFET gates (e.g., LM3880 or discrete TSM/FET network) to eliminate clock noise.
3.  **Voltage Derating:** Update the BOM for the Output Lowpass Filter capacitors to **250V** rating minimum.
4.  **Thermal Design:** Increase heatsink capacity. Current selection leads to thermal shutdown at high ambient temperatures. Target <0.8°C/W thermal resistance.
