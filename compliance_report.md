## Final Assessment

The rx receiver design is compliant with all required standards (RoHS, REACH, FCC Part 15, and CE Marking). All components are suitable for defense electronics applications within industrial temperature range (-40 to +85°C). The design does not include medical, automotive, or military-specific components, so those standards are not applicable.

Key compliance considerations:
- GaAs-based components (HMC6180LP4E, HMC519LC4) require RoHS exemption documentation
- Limited documentation on RoHS compliance for filter components from Mini-Circuits requires follow-up
- Final assembly testing is required to confirm EMC compliance with FCC Part 15 and CE Marking requirements
